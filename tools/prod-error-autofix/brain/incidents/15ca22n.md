**Mechanism.** articleController.update (and genAIBlogService.updateBlog) put seoProxyApi({url:'/updateOvrList', ...}) into a Promise.all alongside the real Shopify write. seoProxyApi wraps the axios call in try/catch (helpers/api.js:81) and its catch has no rethrow and no return, so a rejected upstream call resolves to undefined and the Promise.all completes normally — the article update still succeeds.
